# Outline 2: Why one habit stuck and another didn't

- **Working title:** Why one habit stuck for me and another one didn't
- **Category:** Habits  |  **Slug:** `why-one-habit-stuck-and-another-did-not`
- **Reader:** someone who keeps starting habits and dropping them.
- **Promise:** a side-by-side comparison from real experience that helps the reader diagnose their own habit.

## Structure
1. **Opening:** name the two habits (one that stuck, one that didn't) and how long each lasted.
2. **The habit that stuck.** When, where, how big, what triggered it, what made it easy. Be specific and measurable (time, place, duration).
3. **The habit that didn't.** Same questions. Where did it break down, and when did you notice?
4. **The differences.** A short table: trigger, size, effort, who else was involved, what happened on a bad day.
5. **What I'd do differently.** One concrete adjustment you'd make to the failed habit.
6. **A diagnostic for you:** 4 or 5 questions the reader can ask about their own habit.

## From your experience
- What is the smallest version of the habit that still counts?
- What was happening in your life when the failed habit ended?
- Did you ever restart it? What changed?
