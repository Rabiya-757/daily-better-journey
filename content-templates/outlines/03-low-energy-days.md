# Outline 3: A minimum version for low-energy days

- **Working title:** What to do on days you don't feel like it: build a minimum version of your routine
- **Category:** Motivation  |  **Slug:** `minimum-version-for-low-energy-days`
- **Reader:** someone whose routine collapses the first time they're tired, sick or busy.
- **Promise:** a practical way to define a "minimum version" so a bad day doesn't end a streak.

## Structure
1. **Opening:** a real day you almost skipped everything.
2. **The all-or-nothing trap.** How it showed up for you.
3. **Define your minimum.** For each routine (exercise, writing, study, tidy-up) write the 2-minute and 10-minute version. *Your example:* a table of your own routines and their minimum versions.
4. **Rules for using it.** When you may drop to the minimum, and when you may not.
5. **What I noticed.** Did the minimum version lead to doing more? Sometimes it didn't. Say so.
6. **Try it:** have the reader write their own minimum versions in five minutes.

## From your experience
- Which routine did you most often abandon, and what was its minimum version?
- How did you feel after doing only the minimum?
- Was there a day the minimum wasn't realistic? What did you do?
