# Outline 4: A 20-minute weekly review

- **Working title:** A 20-minute weekly review: what to look at and what to ignore
- **Category:** Productivity  |  **Slug:** `twenty-minute-weekly-review`
- **Reader:** someone with tasks and commitments scattered across notes, email and memory.
- **Promise:** a short review routine the reader can run every week without a special tool.

## Structure
1. **Opening:** what happened the week you skipped planning.
2. **When and where.** Your day, time and setting. Keep it to one place.
3. **The 20 minutes, step by step.** Example split: look back (5), look at the calendar ahead (5), choose three priorities (5), clear small loose ends (5). Replace with your own.
4. **What I ignore on purpose.** Things you've stopped tracking, and why.
5. **A real example.** Show a (lightly edited) before/after of one of your weekly lists.
6. **When the review gets skipped.** What you do the next day.
7. **Template:** a copyable checklist.

## From your experience
- Which tool do you use (paper, notes app, spreadsheet) and why?
- What did you stop doing after you started reviewing?
- How many priorities can you realistically handle in one week?
