# Five article outlines

These are **outlines, not articles**. Each one gives you a structure and prompts. The value of the finished article has to come from **your own experience, your own examples and your own words**.

Guidance that keeps the blog original (and keeps you within AdSense policy expectations):

- Write from something you actually did, tried or observed. If you haven't tried a method yet, try it first, then write.
- Don't state statistics, studies or quotes unless you can name and link the real source.
- Don't publish text you didn't write or substantially rewrite yourself. Copied or lightly spun text is the fastest way to a rejection.
- Give each article a different, specific purpose. Five articles that answer five different questions beat fifty near-duplicates.
- Link to your other articles where it helps the reader (fill the `related:` list in front matter too).

How to use one: run `npm run new -- "Your title"`, then paste the outline headings into the new draft and fill them in.
