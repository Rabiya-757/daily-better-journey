# Outline 5: A one-week notification experiment

- **Working title:** A one-week notification experiment: the setup, what changed and what didn't
- **Category:** Focus  |  **Slug:** `one-week-notification-experiment`
- **Reader:** someone who checks their phone constantly and wants a low-risk way to test changes.
- **Promise:** an honest experiment log the reader can copy.

## Structure
1. **Opening:** how often you were checking your phone and how you knew (screen-time numbers from your own device, if you have them).
2. **The rules.** Exactly which notifications stayed on, which went off, and any exceptions (family, work on-call).
3. **The log.** A short table by day: what you changed, what you noticed, how focused you felt (your own 1-5 rating is fine if you say that's what it is).
4. **What improved.** Only what you actually observed.
5. **What didn't improve, or got worse.** For example, missed messages or anxiety.
6. **What I kept.** The settings you kept after the week.
7. **Run your own week:** a checklist.

## From your experience
- What were your screen-time numbers before and after?
- Who complained about slow replies, if anyone?
- What would you do differently in a second experiment?
