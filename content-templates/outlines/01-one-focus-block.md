# Outline 1: One focus block that actually works

- **Working title:** How to set up one distraction-free focus block (and what to do when it falls apart)
- **Category:** Focus  |  **Slug:** `one-focus-block-that-works`
- **Reader:** someone whose workday is mostly interruptions and who wants one protected stretch of real work.
- **Promise:** a repeatable setup for a single focus block, plus a plan for when it fails.

## Structure
1. **Opening:** describe a specific day when you got to the end with nothing important finished.
2. **Choose the one task.** How you decide what deserves the block. *Your example:* the actual task you picked this week and why.
3. **Set the container.** Length, time of day, where you work, what is closed or silenced. *Your example:* your real setup, including what you tried and dropped.
4. **Start rule.** What you do in the first five minutes so you actually begin.
5. **When it fails.** Interruptions, bad days, shortened blocks. Write down what you do in each case.
6. **What I'd change.** Honest results after trying it for some days or weeks.
7. **Try it this week:** a 4-line plan the reader can copy.

## From your experience (answer these before you write)
- What was your longest uninterrupted block before, and after?
- What was the single biggest cause of broken blocks?
- Which "productivity rule" did you try that didn't suit you?
